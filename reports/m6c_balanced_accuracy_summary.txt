ORDINAL DECODING
Replacing argmax with the expected grade sum_k k*p_k, cut by four thresholds fitted on the validation split to maximise balanced_accuracy, changed resnet50_weighted on the held-out test split from 0.7964 to 0.7745 exact-match accuracy and from 0.8773 to 0.8681 QWK. A paired bootstrap of the accuracy change gives -0.0218 (95% CI [-0.0436, +0.0000]), which includes zero, so it is within sampling noise; the QWK change is -0.0092 (95% CI [-0.0224, +0.0036]). No retraining was involved: the thresholds are four numbers fitted to predictions that already existed, which makes this the cheapest accuracy gain available to the project and a natural baseline for the trained ordinal head.
