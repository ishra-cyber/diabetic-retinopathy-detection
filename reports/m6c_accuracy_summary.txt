ORDINAL DECODING
Replacing argmax with the expected grade sum_k k*p_k, cut by four thresholds fitted on the validation split to maximise accuracy, changed ensemble_gmean on the held-out test split from 0.8036 to 0.8164 exact-match accuracy and from 0.8935 to 0.9011 QWK. A paired bootstrap of the accuracy change gives +0.0127 (95% CI [-0.0164, +0.0418]), which includes zero, so it is within sampling noise; the QWK change is +0.0076 (95% CI [-0.0087, +0.0249]). No retraining was involved: the thresholds are four numbers fitted to predictions that already existed, which makes this the cheapest accuracy gain available to the project and a natural baseline for the trained ordinal head.
